# Trail camera buying comparison

Researched September 10, 2026. US prices, before tax and shipping. Target is 10 to 20 cameras at approximately $100 each, including rechargeable batteries. No purchase or vendor contact has been made.

The required functions are interval photography, useful image quality in low light, motion triggering, removable SD storage, nearby phone live view for aiming, no cellular connection, and a substantial rechargeable battery. I treated solar charging as optional. It cannot be assumed to sustain cameras in shaded deployments. Full-size SD is preferred for continuity with the existing workflow. MicroSD alternatives are identified explicitly.

## Recommendation

GardePro remains the strongest documented fit near this budget. The A80 Pro is my first candidate because its manufacturer identifies a Sony STARVIS sensor and explicitly documents nearby live view. The E8 2.0 Pro is the lower-cost candidate. Neither is a proven image-quality or triggering winner from this research. I did not find comparable controlled measurements for these exact models.

Buy one A80 Pro and one E8 2.0 Pro for a direct comparison before selecting the remaining units. At current single-camera prices that comparison costs $219.98 before tax, cards, and shipping. My decision rule would be to choose A80 Pro if its twilight photographs retain visibly more usable subject detail. Otherwise choose E8 2.0 Pro and use the savings for storage and spare batteries. This is a proposed purchasing approach, not an order placed.

## Functions confirmed in the manuals

The A80 Pro manual confirms individual interval photos or videos, a 3-second to 24-hour interval, and a hybrid mode combining scheduled captures with PIR triggering. It supports full-size SDHC/SDXC to 512GB. Its app has live preview for aiming. Motion detection pauses during the Wi-Fi connection and resumes after disconnection or the documented app timeout. The pack is removable. These details make it a strong functional match, while battery replacement availability remains a separate question. [A80 Pro manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0722/0192/8993/files/GardePro_A80_Pro_Instruction_Manual_V1.1_EN_DE_FR_IT_ES_20250415.pdf?v=1768382900).

The E6 and E8 2.0 Pro manuals also document live preview, full-size SD to 512GB, removable packs, interval photography, and hybrid operation. This matters because a product advertising both time-lapse and motion detection does not necessarily support them at the same time. [E6 manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0722/0192/8993/files/GardePro_E6_2.0_Pro_Instruction_Manual_V1.1_EN_FR_20251011.pdf?v=1768381167), [E8 manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0722/0192/8993/files/GardePro_E8_2.0_Pro_Instruction_Manual_V1.2_EN_FR_20251122.pdf?v=1768381501).

## What remains uncertain

There is no verified best camera on every requested dimension. The recommendation favors documented functions, storage compatibility, substantial batteries, and price. Manufacturer megapixel counts and trigger-speed claims do not establish resolved detail, moving-subject sharpness, missed-trigger rates, or seasonal reliability. The available evidence is insufficient to assign numerical quality scores to these models.

For the sample comparison, use identical framing and the intended subject distance. Compare original JPEGs in daylight, twilight, and infrared, including moving subjects. Check live aiming without internet, disconnect the app, and verify normal capture resumes. Run the actual 10-minute schedule overnight and check timestamps, image counts, clock persistence after a battery swap, and whether motion events disrupt scheduled captures. The existing duration script can help assess recording coverage once a matching inventory exists, but its spans alone do not prove battery exhaustion.

If the target remains monarchs, do not assume a PIR speed claim guarantees insect detection. Keep interval photography as the dependable sampling method and validate motion capture separately. The A80 manual itself identifies scheduled capture as useful for ectotherms. Close-focus distance also needs verification at the actual installation distance.

Before a fleet purchase, get written confirmation of the supplied battery's watt-hours, its separately purchasable replacement, the shipped sensor/hardware revision, and warranty handling for multiple units. Request original twilight and night JPEGs at the intended subject distance if available. These are procurement questions, not a reason to assume the cameras cannot work.
