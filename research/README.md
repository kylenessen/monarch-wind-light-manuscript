# Review and provenance records

These are dated records of work supporting the manuscript and observational release. Read the manuscript and analysis guides first. Historical records describe their state on the date shown and may be superseded by later records.

The [final publication review](final-publication-review-2026-09-22.md) records numerical and editorial checks. The [submission package record](submission-package-2026-09-24.md) identifies the final ZIP and its verification. The [USGS approval notes](usgs-manuscript-approval-2026-09-16.md), [release review](data-release-review-2026-09-16.md) and [ScienceBase package inventory](sciencebase-upload-summary-2026-09-15.md) document release preparation.

[Operations](operations/README.md) contains historical SSD transfers, recovery inventories and camera purchasing notes. These records are retained for provenance and are not instructions for reproducing the manuscript's analyses.
