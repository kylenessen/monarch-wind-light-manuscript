# Submission Portal Responses

These paragraphs are concise summaries for the three response fields in the MDPI revision portal. The attached response letter provides the complete point-by-point replies. The final paragraph is a confidential note for the editorial office if the portal provides an appropriate field.

## Academic Editor

Thank you for inviting us to revise the manuscript. We substantially shortened the paper and narrowed its interpretation to an observational study of two groves during one overwintering season. We removed the extended discussion of monarch population decline, unsupported physiological claims, prescriptive management recommendations, redundant analyses, and several technical figures and tables. The revised title and all summary sections now state that the predicted consistent decline was not detected under the monitored conditions. The attached response letter describes each change in detail.

## Reviewer 1

Thank you for the careful review. We addressed every comment in the manuscript and in the attached point-by-point response. We restricted the scope of inference, clarified the historical framing, sensor placement, wind metric, sun-exposure measure, camera temperatures, Butterfly Index, and biological unit of replication, and removed unsupported claims about grounded butterflies and physiological mechanisms. We also corrected the model-comparison workflow. We removed duplicate candidates, used maximum likelihood and AICc for comparison, refitted selected models with restricted maximum likelihood, and added separate sensitivity analyses. All 121 fits in the revised primary and sensitivity comparisons completed without convergence warnings. The processed data, model formulas, analysis code, and model outputs are public. The classification protocol and software are also public, and the associated USGS ScienceBase data release is in preparation for release before publication.

## Reviewer 2

Thank you for the constructive comments. We added photographs of both study groves and revised the manuscript throughout to emphasize that the study covers two localities and one season. We clarified that the nearby wind measurements may not represent wind exposure at butterfly positions within the canopy. We also removed broad management recommendations and now present the findings as questions for replication across more sites, seasons, grove structures, cluster densities, and geographic regions. The attached response letter identifies the corresponding manuscript revisions for each comment.
