# M16 role-permutation figures

These figures show all six assignments of maximum wind gust, temperature, and butterflies visible in direct sun to the x-axis, line color, and figure panels. All figures use the same fitted M16 model and hold previous BI at its sample median of 37.

Temperature slices are 10, 15, and 20 degrees C. Wind slices are its observed 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles, which are 1.3, 2.2, and 3.0 m/s. Direct-sun slices are 0, 3, and 20 butterflies, representing none and the 25th and 75th percentiles among nonzero observations.

Continuous wind axes end at the overall 99th percentile of 6.21 m/s. Continuous temperature axes span 10 to 20 degrees C. Continuous direct-sun axes span 0 to 20 butterflies. Within those shared limits, each line is restricted to the minimum-to-maximum x range among the 50 observations nearest to its fixed line and panel conditions. All observations remain in model fitting.

The overview image places all six permutations on one sheet. The six individual images provide larger versions for closer inspection. Prediction data and displayed support ranges are stored in the M16 tables directory.
