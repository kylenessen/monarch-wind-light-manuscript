The 30-minute analysis used 1894 paired observations from 115 monitoring periods at 2 overwintering sites during the 2023-2024 season. We compared 50 unique candidate models using maximum likelihood. Of these, 49 produced rankable fits. Model M16 ranked first and included Previous butterfly count (linear), Interaction: Maximum wind speed x Temperature x Butterflies in direct sun. It had an AIC of 8055.947, an Akaike weight of 0.9997, and a Delta AIC of 17.5 relative to the next-ranked model. Model M16 was then refitted using restricted maximum likelihood for coefficient estimation.
